Who we are

Growing food together

Riverbend is a volunteer-run community garden: forty plots for neighbours, a shared herb bed, and a harvest table that feeds whoever needs it. It is made up — a demo of what Patchlamp builds for a nonprofit, a church or an event.

Plots are free; the tools, water and seed swap run on volunteers and small gifts.
